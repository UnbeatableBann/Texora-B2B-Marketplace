# User Personas

## Purpose

This document defines the primary user personas for the Textile Marketplace.

These personas guide product decisions, user experience, personalization, AI interactions, and future feature development.

The marketplace is designed around two primary user groups:

* Buyers
* Suppliers

Every feature implemented should solve a real problem for at least one of these personas.

---

# Persona 1 — Buyer

## Overview

The Buyer is a business looking to discover, evaluate, and purchase textile products from multiple suppliers.

The buyer values:

* Fast product discovery
* Accurate product information
* Competitive pricing
* Reliable suppliers
* Simple purchasing process

The marketplace should help buyers quickly find suitable fabrics without requiring extensive manual searching.

# Buyer Goals

The buyer wants to:

# Buyer Pain Points

Current challenges include:

* Searching across multiple suppliers.
* Inconsistent product information.
* Difficulty comparing fabrics.
* Time-consuming supplier communication.
* Unclear inventory availability.
* Finding products within budget.
* Discovering alternatives when products are unavailable.

---

# Buyer Needs

The marketplace should help buyers:

* Search products efficiently.
* Filter products accurately.
* Compare products.
* Understand product specifications.
* Receive AI-powered recommendations.
* Complete purchases with minimal friction.

---

# Buyer Technical Experience

* Business websites

* E-commerce platforms

The interface should be intuitive without requiring technical expertise.

# Buyer Success Criteria

* Place an order without confusion.
* Receive relevant recommendations.
* Track orders easily.

# Design Principles Derived from Personas

Every feature should satisfy one or more of the following principles:

* Reduce the time required to complete common tasks.
* Present only relevant information for the user's role.
* Minimize unnecessary steps.
* Keep workflows intuitive and predictable.
* Use AI to simplify decisions, not complicate them.
* Prioritize clarity, speed, and usability over feature quantity.

These personas should be treated as the primary reference for designing user flows, dashboards, AI interactions, and future enhancements.
